My Money — Terms of Use

Last updated: 8 November 2026

My Money is a personal budgeting application being developed for private, non-commercial use. It is not currently offered as a public service.

Purpose

The application is intended to display financial information and estimate amounts available for discretionary spending based on account balances, transactions, anticipated bills, and user-defined budgets.

Financial information and estimates

Budgets, forecasts, and “safe to spend” figures are estimates and may be incomplete, delayed, or inaccurate. They are not guarantees of available funds and do not constitute financial, investment, or other professional advice. Account balances and obligations should be checked directly with the relevant financial institutions before making decisions.

Bank connections

Any future connection to a bank or financial institution will require the account holder’s authorisation and will be subject to the applicable bank and account-information provider terms. My Money is intended to use read-only account information, not initiate payments.

Availability and changes

The application is under development. Its features, calculations, and availability may change, and it may contain errors. No commitment is made to provide uninterrupted service.

Privacy

Information about the intended handling of personal data is available in the My Money Privacy Policy.

Contact

For enquiries about this personal application, use the contact email supplied to the account-information provider during application registration.
