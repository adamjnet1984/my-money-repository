My Money Personal — Privacy Notice

Last updated: 8 November 2026

My Money Personal is a private, personal-use budgeting application in development. It is not currently offered to the public. Live bank connections have not yet been enabled.

Purpose

The application is intended to help its owner understand available funds, expected expenses, card liabilities and an estimated amount available for discretionary spending.

Information the application may process

When bank connections are enabled with the account holder's consent, the application is intended to process account balances, transaction dates, descriptions and amounts, relevant account identifiers, and information about card balances or repayments made available by the connected institution. It may also process user-entered budgets, savings allowances and spending classifications.

How information will be used

Information will be used to classify transactions, estimate future expenditure, identify recurring payments and calculate a personal budgeting forecast. The application is intended to use read-only account access and will not initiate payments.

Bank connections and third parties

If enabled, account access will be authorised through Enable Banking and the relevant financial institution. Their own privacy notices also apply to their services. A secure application backend may be used to retrieve and process authorised account data. The backend provider and hosting arrangements have not yet been selected; this notice will be updated before live financial information is processed.

Storage and security

At present, the prototype uses fictional data. Before connecting real accounts, the application will be configured to protect access credentials and financial data, including keeping private API keys out of the mobile application. Specific storage, retention and deletion arrangements will be documented here before live use.

Sharing

The application is not intended to sell financial information or use it for advertising. Data may need to be processed by the selected banking connectivity and hosting providers to operate the service.

Access, withdrawal and deletion

The account holder will be able to withdraw bank-connection consent through the relevant provider or bank. Data access and deletion procedures will be established before live use.

Contact

For privacy enquiries, use the contact email supplied during the application's Enable Banking registration.

This notice describes a personal development project and will be updated before any live bank connection is activated.
